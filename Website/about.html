ABOUT · CAPSICOM

Built to keep communication simple.

Messages and files, device to device.

Capsi is a lightweight utility for computers that need to communicate directly. It focuses on finding nearby devices, sending messages, and moving files without putting a cloud service in the middle.

The Capsi approach

Capsi is intentionally small. It is not trying to become another social platform, account system, or always-online workspace.

Connect the computers. Run Capsi. Let them find each other. Decide which devices to accept. Then send what you need.

What Capsi does

  • Discovers nearby Capsi devices on the connected local network.
  • Sends one-to-one messages between accepted devices.
  • Transfers files after the recipient accepts the offer.
  • Keeps conversation history locally on the computer.

What Capsi avoids

  • No account or signup flow.
  • No Capsi cloud chat service for local communication.
  • No social feed or unnecessary platform features.
  • No claim that local networking makes a device invisible or anonymous.
