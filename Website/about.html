About Capsi

Messages and files, device to device.

Capsi is a lightweight local-network communication and file-transfer utility by CAPSICOM. Computers on the same Wi-Fi or wired network discover each other and communicate directly — no cloud chat service, no accounts. It is a utility for nearby computers, not a social or cloud messaging platform.

What it does

  • Lists nearby computers running Capsi on the same local network.
  • Sends one-to-one text messages between devices you have accepted.
  • Sends files (one per offer, up to 100 MB) to a device you have accepted, after the other side accepts the file.
  • Keeps per-device conversation history stored locally on your computer.

What it does not do

  • No cloud chat service for local communication; downloads come from GitHub releases.
  • No accounts, signup, or login — identity is a device name plus a locally stored key.
  • No social feed, groups, or extras; no claims about encryption, anonymity, or cross-network reach — see open questions below.
