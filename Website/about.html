About Capsi

Send Messages and files, device to device.

Capsi is a lightweight on-premises / local-network communication and file-transfer utility by CAPSICOM. It lets computers on the same local network discover each other and communicate directly, without cloud services, external servers, or user accounts.

What it does

  • Discovers compatible Capsi devices on the local network.
  • Sends messages directly between devices.
  • Sends files directly from one device to another.

What it does not do

  • No cloud service required for local communication.
  • No accounts, signup, or login.
  • No social feed or unnecessary extras.
