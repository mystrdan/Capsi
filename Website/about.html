ABOUT CAPSI

Communication doesn't always need a middleman.

Messages and files, device to device.

Capsi is a lightweight utility for computers that need to communicate directly. It helps connected devices find one another, exchange messages, and move files without putting a cloud service in the middle.

THE IDEA

When your devices can reach each other, they should be able to talk to each other.

Capsi keeps communication close to the devices involved. There is no signup flow to work through and no online workspace to maintain just to send something from one computer to another.

The connection can be a LAN, Wi-Fi, mobile hotspot, or another supported local network. Capsi works with the connection you already have.

HOW IT WORKS

Run. Find. Send.

There is very little to configure.

  1. Run

    Open Capsi on the computers you want to connect.

  2. Find

    Capsi discovers other computers running Capsi on the connected local network.

  3. Send

    Accept a device, then send messages and files directly between the computers.

WHAT IT DOES

The useful parts. Nothing extra.

Messages

Send one-to-one text messages between accepted devices.

Files

Offer files to another device and let the recipient accept or decline.

Devices

Discover nearby computers running Capsi without manually entering addresses.

Local history

Keep conversation history locally on the computer rather than relying on a cloud chat service.

LOCAL FIRST

Your devices. Your network. Your data.

Capsi does not need an external chat service to move messages and files between connected devices.

  • No account or signup flow
  • No Capsi cloud service for local communication
  • No internet connection required for local communication
  • Conversation history stays on the device

Capsi is a local communication utility, not a promise of anonymity. Your network still has its own rules, visibility, and controls.
