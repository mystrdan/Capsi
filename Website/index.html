THE IDEA

Communication doesn't always need a middleman.

Capsi is built around a simple idea: when your devices can reach each other, they should be able to talk to each other.

Your local setup can be Wi-Fi, wired Ethernet, a hotspot, or another connected local network. Capsi is there to make the computers find one another and send.

SIMPLE BY DESIGN

How it works

There is very little to set up. Install it, open it, and let Capsi do the finding.

  1. Run

    Install Capsi on the computers you want to connect and give each device a name.

  2. Find

    Capsi discovers other computers running Capsi on the connected local network.

  3. Send

    Accept a device, then send messages and files directly between the computers.

WHAT YOU GET

No Account

LOCAL FIRST

Your devices. Your network. Your data.

Capsi does not need an external chat service to move messages and files between connected devices.

  • No cloud account
  • No login or registration
  • No internet connection required for local communication
  • Conversation history stays on the device

Capsi is not presented as a privacy or anonymity tool. Your network still has its own rules, visibility, and controls.

Capsi by CAPSICOM is a lightweight Windows utility for sending messages and files directly between computers on a connected local network.

No internet connection is required for local device-to-device communication. You only need internet when you need to obtain Capsi or access online services outside the local connection.
